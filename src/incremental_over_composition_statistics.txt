Incremental value of panel over cell-type composition — experiment #2
================================================================
Substrate: raw log1p GPL24676 (n=874 ALS+control); LightGBM top-500
params; 5-fold stratified CV (random_state=42); BRETIGEA composition.

Model                                        AUC              95% CI
--------------------------------------------------------------------
A. Composition only (6 cell types)        0.7278   [0.687, 0.768]
B. Panel only (25 genes)                  0.9235   [0.903, 0.941]
C. Panel + composition                    0.9264   [0.907, 0.944]
D. Panel residualised on composition      0.8906   [0.863, 0.914]

Panel-only minus composition-only : +0.1957
Residualised panel (composition removed): 0.8906

Interpretation:
  - Panel (0.923) >> composition alone (0.728): the panel is not reducible to cell-type proportions.
  - After regressing out composition, the panel still discriminates at AUC=0.891, i.e. it carries per-cell / non-composition signal.
  - Adding composition to the panel changes AUC by +0.0029 (composition adds little once the panel is present).
